# Forkcast promo: storyboard (approved, with the skill's 3 tweaks)

**Length:** about 24 seconds · **Format:** vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920), for phones, Instagram Reels / TikTok / Stories · **Built with:** Remotion (videos made from React code), exported as an .mp4

## Rules for every shot

- **Everything happens in the dead center.** Your eyes never have to hunt around the screen.
- **Each shot transforms into the next.** No fades between shots: one shape always becomes the next one.
- **Always some motion.** A slow camera drift and gentle zoom, even in quiet moments.
- **Text:** short sentence-case lines; each word rises from blurry to sharp, one after another. No bouncing.
- **The real Forkcast look:** warm off-white background `#F6F5F2`, white cards, near-black text and buttons, periwinkle ring, sage green for "on target," sky/butter/lilac macros, Barlow Condensed headlines and Inter text. The app screens are rebuilt from the real app's colors and checked against real screenshots so nothing looks fake.
- **A soft blur behind the action** (shallow depth of field), so it looks filmed.
