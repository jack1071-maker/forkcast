# Forkcast promo: storyboard v2 (adds a 3D phone made in Blender)

**Length:** about 28 seconds · **Format:** vertical 9:16 (1080 × 1920) · **Built with:** Remotion (2D shots) + Blender (3D phone shots), joined into one .mp4

The approved v1 shots stay. Two moments become 3D, using a modern phone built in Blender: black glass front, thin bezel,
titanium frame, Dynamic Island, a triple camera on the back. No Apple logo or branding, and no side buttons (they're hard to
place exactly). The app's real screens are mapped onto its display.

**3D look:** a light studio that matches Forkcast's warm off-white, one big soft light (that's most of what makes it look
cinematic), a soft shadow under the phone, and a long lens with the background out of focus.

## Sound (after you approve the picture)
Soft pops on shape changes, a camera-shutter click at the photo, light ticks on counting numbers, airy whooshes only under the
phone's spin and the camera moves, a gentle swell and low hit on the end card, plus a calm music bed.
